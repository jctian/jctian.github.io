abstract: >-
  This paper studies how incorporating a weaker surface rheology influences the tectonics of Venus. In this study, we identified a new tectonic regime, the "deformable episodic lid", that is characterized by continuous surface movement with episodic global overturns. We also found a new resurfacing mechanism under stagnant-lid regime caused by global volcanism due to internal mantle overturns. These newly found tectonic regimes provide single, physically consistent mechanisms for both the large-scale resurfacing events that explain the planet's young surface age and the continuous geologic activity observed today.
